Database and System Flow

Visual map of the updated applicant journey from account access to final HR notification.

1. Access and References

Users authenticate as applicants or HR, while education and skill catalogs standardize model inputs.

users educations skills

2. Job Criteria

HR creates job posts with minimum education, age, experience, and required skills.

jobs job_required_skills

3. CART Screening

Applications capture profile data, resume files, skills, experience, and eligibility output.

applications applicant_skills experiences

4. ANN Interview

Eligible candidates answer job-matched questions scored by cosine, keyword, and ANN metrics.

interview_questions interview_sessions interview_answers

5. Decision Output

HR reviews final qualification, approves or rejects applicants, and sends platform alerts.

notifications HR decision

Screening Decision Lane

CART Eligibility Compares applicant education, age, years of experience, and skill matches against the job baseline.
ANN Qualification Uses answer similarity, keyword density, and final answer scores to compute interview readiness.
HR Action Turns the model outputs into Approved, Rejected, or follow-up communication for the applicant.

Updated Database Modules

Core References

  • users: login identity and role
  • educations: degree catalog with CART value
  • skills: universal skill dictionary

Job Posting

  • jobs: position and screening requirements
  • job_required_skills: job-to-skill bridge

Application Screening

  • applications: resume and CART result
  • applicant_skills: applicant skill bridge
  • experiences: structured work history

Interview Simulation

  • interview_questions: job-based question pool
  • interview_sessions: ANN average and status
  • interview_answers: answer-level NLP scores

System Utility

  • notifications: applicant and HR alerts
  • is_read: read tracking for UI feedback
